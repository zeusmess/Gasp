# Storyboard — "She stepped out of the circle"

**Idea:** every October the world turns pink. This year the Starbucks Siren steps out of her green circle as a 3D figure and embraces the women of the event. Her twin tails become arms that wrap around each photo. At the end every photo and moment joins into one pink ribbon, with the logo resting in its loop.

Format 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 52.8 s, 100 BPM (bar = 2.4 s). Key text sits in the 250–560 px band at the top, clear of the Reels/TikTok UI.
