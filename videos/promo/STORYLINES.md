# Personal promo: storylines

Assets: `a4e66b67-image.png` (black-and-white film portrait, blind-stripe light, black blazer and turtleneck) and `5bf9e100-image.png` (studio sheet: front, 3/4, profiles, back, eye close-up).
Voiceover: Egyptian Arabic, first person, ElevenLabs. Length 25–35 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps.

## A. Light through the blinds (recommended)
Device: the result first. Form: one continuous take.
Opens on a white wall with stripes of light; the stripes slide aside and the black-and-white portrait builds out of them. The camera never cuts: it pushes into the eyes, which turn into the eye close-up from the studio sheet, then pulls back through the angles (3/4, profile, back) as one turning head. Ends back on the portrait, name and CTA wiping in on a stripe of light.
First 2 s: a bright wall, a single stripe of light moves, a voice says the first line.
Beats: wall/light hook → face built by light → eyes (who I am) → the turning angles (what I do) → full portrait payoff → name + CTA.
Look: warm white wall, deep black, film grain from the portrait itself, one accent colour on the name only.
Sound: soft piano/ambient pulse, a shutter click on each reveal.

## B. The contact sheet
Device: a question with the input. Form: split screen to one.
All seven frames of the studio sheet play as panes; each answers one question ("مين؟", "بيعمل إيه؟", ...), then they all collapse into the black-and-white portrait.
Beats: grid hook → each pane answers → collapse → name + CTA.
Look: neutral grey studio ground, black type. Sound: camera clicks on the beat, a light beat.

## C. Type is the face (bold)
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant Egyptian Arabic words fill the frame and cut every half beat; the photos appear only inside the letters, until the last word opens and becomes the full portrait.
Beats: words → face in letters → full face → name + CTA.
Look: pure black and white, very large Arabic type. Sound: a punchy beat, hits on every cut.
